New lesson: make a video

Type a school topic and press Make video: the class chat model writes the scenes, the image model draws the pictures, the voice model speaks them, and this page records the video (about 2 minutes, keep the tab open).

Upload your own video

Made a video on your phone or in another app? Add the file here (mp4, mov, webm, mkv, up to 15 minutes). It becomes a lesson in the list with your grade, and everyone in class can watch it.
